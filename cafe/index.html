Chef

Welcome to your cafe

Run it like the classic cafe games: cook, serve, clean, decorate, and keep the buzz up.

Cook
Tap a stove, pick a dish, then tap it three times to prep. Your chef walks over and does each step.
Serve
When it's done, tap it to move it to a counter. You get the dish's XP then. Waiters carry it to guests.
Clean
Stoves need cleaning before the next dish.
Seats
One guest per chair, and a chair needs a table in front of it.
Buzz
Fed guests raise it, guests who leave hungry drop it. Higher buzz brings more people in.
Grow
Levels unlock recipes, more stoves, counters and staff, plus decor and expansions.
Walk
Phone: left thumb moves, right thumb looks. Computer: click to look, WASD to move, E to use.
